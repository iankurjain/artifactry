The problem

The token limit isn't the problem. Starting from zero is.

You build the battlecard in Claude until the chat runs out, finish the demo in Cursor, and ask a teammate to make the deck in Gemini. Every switch drops what the last tool knew, so the same story comes out five different ways.

01

The good version is buried in a chat

Last week's launch plan lives in someone's ChatGPT history. Nobody can find it, so it gets written again, slightly differently.

02

Every handoff loses the context

Copy, paste, re-explain. The next AI never sees the research, the decisions or the customer's own words, and guesses the rest.

03

Quality depends on who prompted

Same product, same customer, three teammates, three tools: different numbers, different claims, different tone. The customer notices.

How a handoff works

One asset, three AIs, zero re‑explaining.

A solutions engineer preps a strategic account. Each tool does the part it's best at, and each one starts from what the last one made.

  1. Claude · research

    Research the account, then save it

    "Save this research to Artifactry and hand it to Cursor to build the demo."

    The report is saved as v1 with a permanent link, tagged from its content, and labelled made by Claude. The handoff carries a brief written for the next tool.

  2. Cursor · build

    Pick up the handoff and build

    "Open my Artifactry handoff."

    Cursor reads the brief and the research in full, builds the demo, and saves the code and a technical spec that link back to the research they were built from.

  3. Gemini · present

    Turn it into the deck

    "Make the executive deck from the Acme research and spec in Artifactry."

    The deck starts from the real numbers and the real spec, not a paraphrase. It's saved next to them in the same project.

  4. Next week · anyone, any tool

    Find it, reuse it, improve it

    "Find last week's Acme deck and make a version for Globex."

    Search reads what's inside every file. The Globex deck becomes v1 of a new artifact, built from the Acme one, so you can always trace where a claim came from.

Why it compounds

Chat history resets. Your second brain compounds.

Every artifact you save makes the next one better. On day one Artifactry is a library. By month three it holds your approved positioning, your best demos, your winning decks and the proof points behind them, and every AI on the team starts from there.

  • Your best work becomes the starting point. Ask any AI to build from what's already approved instead of from a blank prompt.
  • Consistency without a style police. When everyone's tools draw on the same source, the numbers, claims and voice line up on their own.
  • Every claim is traceable. Lineage shows which artifacts each one was built from, and which AI made each version.
  • It belongs to the team, not a tool. Switch models next quarter and the brain stays exactly where it is.
