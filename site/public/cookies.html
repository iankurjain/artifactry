1. What cookies are

Cookies are small text files a website stores in your browser. Similar technologies, like local storage, work the same way. Some are essential for a site to work; others remember preferences or measure how a site is used.

2. This website

This marketing website sets no cookies of its own and runs no analytics or advertising trackers.

NameTypePurposeLasts
artifactry-themeLocal storage (functional)Remembers whether you chose light or dark mode. Never sent to us.Until you clear it

Third parties may set their own cookies when their features load:

  • Cloudflare, our host, may set strictly necessary security cookies (such as bot-protection cookies) to tell people from automated traffic.
  • Splitforms loads a script on the Request a Feature page and the home page and receives your form submission. It may use its own cookies or storage for spam protection.
  • Google Fonts serves our fonts. It doesn't set cookies for this, but it does receive your IP address.

3. The Artifactry app

The app at app.artifactry.app uses only strictly necessary cookies:

NamePurposeLasts
al_sessionKeeps you signed in. Secure, HTTP-only.Up to 30 days, or until you log out
al_shareRemembers that a recipient verified their email to open a share.12 hours

Because these are essential for signing in and opening shares, they can't be switched off in the app.

4. Analytics cookies

We don't currently use analytics or advertising cookies on this website or in the app. Usage analytics inside the app are counted on our servers without cookies or fingerprinting. If we add analytics cookies in future, we'll update this policy first and ask for your consent where the law requires it.

5. Controlling cookies

You can block or delete cookies and local storage in your browser settings. Blocking the app's essential cookies will stop you from signing in. Clearing local storage on this website simply resets your theme to dark.

6. Changes and contact

We'll update this policy if what we use changes, and revise the date above. Questions? Email us at admin@artifactry.app.
