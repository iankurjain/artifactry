What makes a great request

  • Start with the problem. "I lose track of which AI made which draft" tells us more than "add a filter".
  • Describe today's workaround. It shows us how much the problem hurts.
  • Mention your AI app. Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini, Cursor or something else.
  • One idea per request. It's easier to track and to tell you when it ships.
