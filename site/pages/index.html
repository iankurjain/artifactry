Why Artifactry

Your AI's work shouldn't vanish when the chat ends.

Every assistant you use makes useful things. Artifactry gives them one place to keep those things, so nothing gets lost in a chat history again.

One library for every AI

Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini and your coding agents all save to the same place, and each can pick up what another made.

Links that never break

Every artifact gets a permanent address. Rename it, move it or add ten new versions, and the link you shared still works.

Find it by what's inside

Search reads the text inside your PDFs, Word files, slides and pages, by keyword and by meaning, and tells you how each result matched.

You stay in charge

An AI can never see more than you can. Sharing outside your organization always waits for your yes, and every action is logged.
