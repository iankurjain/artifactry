# Artifactry positioning and messaging

## Positioning in one line

**Artifactry is the second brain for multi-AI work: every AI on your team saves to it, hands off through it and builds on it, so the work comes out consistent whoever made it.**

## Category

The second brain for multi-AI work. Not "AI memory" (Supermemory, Mem0, Second Brain for AI and built-in ChatGPT/Claude memory store facts for one person) and not a drive or wiki (stores files no AI can hand off). Artifactry sits between them: finished work, shared by a team, moved between tools.

## Who it's for (ICP)

Sales, marketing and solutions engineers who build assets for customers and for each other, across several AI tools at once (often because one tool runs out of tokens or is better at the next step), with teammates, under a deadline.

Their pain, in their words:
- "The good version is somewhere in someone's chat history."
- "Every time I switch tools I paste the context and re-explain."
- "Three of us made the same deck three ways, with different numbers."

## The problem

The token limit isn't the problem. Starting from zero is. Every switch between tools or teammates drops what the last one knew, so quality and consistency depend on who prompted, not on what the team already knows.

## The promise

Every AI on your team. One brain. Claude researches, Cursor builds, Gemini makes the deck, and everything lands in one place: saved, versioned, handed from tool to tool with the brief attached, and found again next week.

## The moat: compounding knowledge

Chat history resets. A second brain compounds. Every saved artifact makes the next one better: by month three the library holds the team's approved positioning, best demos and winning decks, and every AI starts from there. Each switch of model or tool leaves the brain where it is, and lineage shows which artifacts every claim was built from. The longer a team uses it, the more expensive it is to leave and the better its output gets.

## Message pillars (each backed by shipped features)

1. **Save**: "Save this to Artifactry." Versions, permanent links, content-derived tags, files of every kind (docs, decks, code, audio, video).
2. **Hand off**: "Hand this to Gemini for the deck." handoff, get_handoff and list_handoffs carry a brief plus up to 20 artifacts, read in full.
3. **Find**: "Find last week's launch plan." Keyword and semantic search over the text inside every file, with access applied inside the search.
4. **Compound**: provenance ("made by Claude") on every version, lineage ("built from", declared or inferred), projects for multi-tool work.
5. **Governed**: an AI never sees more than its user, outside sharing waits for a yes, per-app OAuth scopes, an audit trail.

## The habit we want

Three sentences said to whichever AI the person already uses: "Save this to Artifactry." "Find last week's launch plan." "Hand this to Gemini for the deck." North-star metric stays cross-tool handoffs per active workspace per week.

## What the research says

- Teams already run several AIs side by side. In Ahrefs' 2026 survey of 301 marketers, 83% use Claude, 76% ChatGPT and 58% Gemini, and 93% use AI to create content ([Relevant Audience summary of Ahrefs](https://www.relevantaudience.com/content-marketing/ahrefs-state-of-ai-content-marketing-2026/)).
- Output still needs a human pass: 89% check AI output by hand and 66% trust it only after review (same source). Consistency is a real, unsolved cost.
- AI memory products (Second Brain for AI, Supermemory, Mem0) position on per-person "memory across tools" ([Product Hunt](https://www.producthunt.com/products/second-brain-cloudflare/launches/second-brain-for-ai-v2)); none centre finished team assets, handoffs or lineage. That's our open lane.
- MCP is turning second-brain apps into something agents read and write, and apps without it become silos ([nocal](https://nocal.app/blog/2026/03/mcp-second-brain-future)).
- Sales enablement has long reported most marketing content goes unused by sales ([Bigtincan](https://bigtincan.com/blog/why-sales-content-is-underutilized), [Seismic](https://seismic.com/newsroom/seismic-featured-in-bizjournals-why-unused-content-can-be-a-silent-killer-for-sales-and-marketing-efforts/)). AI makes more content faster, which makes "find and reuse the approved version" more valuable, not less.

## Claims we deliberately don't make yet

- Automatic context ("your AI reads the library before it starts") is roadmap Phase 3 (get_context); today the AI searches when asked. The site says "ask any AI to build from what's approved".
- No customer logos, numbers or testimonials until we have real ones.
